How we grade condition

Sellers choose one grade per device and must describe any known issues.

GradeWhat it means
Like NewLooks and works as if unused. No visible marks, fully working, usually with box and accessories.
GoodLight signs of use such as small scratches or minor wear. Fully working with no functional faults.
FairClear cosmetic wear such as scuffs or dents. Works as expected, and any known issues are listed.

Tips for a faster sale

  • Photograph the device in daylight, with the screen on.
  • Be upfront about marks. Honest listings get fewer disputes.
  • Include the exact model and storage size in the model field.
  • Check similar listings on the browse page before setting a price.
